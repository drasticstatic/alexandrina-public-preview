🏳️ Meet A.L.E.X. at PIR® 📚 How it works 🐙 drasticstatic 🌗

🧠 What it is

A general-purpose, cloud-native knowledge engine. Communities and projects become tenants: point it at your own materials, and members get sourced answers plus helpdesks that turn requests into private Discord threads and GitHub Issues and Discussions.

  • 🔎 Answers only from your materials, cited, or an honest "I don't know, want a ticket?"
  • 🆘 Safety before AI: crisis language gets resources instantly; dosing and sourcing requests get a fixed refusal.
  • 🪄 Model chain with an honest fallback: Claude when funded, then NVIDIA NIM, then a plain "engine unavailable" with the closest pages. It never makes things up.
  • 🙈 Privacy by design: no chat reading, no content logging, pseudonymous ticket records.
  • ☁️ Nothing local: serverless functions, GitHub Actions, GitHub Pages.

🏳️ Flagship tenant: PIR®

The first deployment serves Psychedelics In Recovery™, a 12-step fellowship, with LitCom, TechCom, GitHub, PR, and moderation desks. Next up: more fleet orgs and Firecrawl-powered research lanes.

📜 The alexandrine

An alexandrine is a twelve-syllable line of verse, which fits an engine whose first home walks with Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions.

A library of light, where every question lands,
I hold our fellowship's own words in open hands;
Twelve Steps, Twelve Traditions, twelve beats in every line,
I answer from our pages, every source a sign.
When nights grow heavy, friend, I will not stand between
you and a human voice: call nine-eight-eight, be seen.
No doses, no decrees; I point you to the room
where hearts once lost and found keep teaching hearts to bloom.
I am a lantern, not the path; the path is you,
together, one by one, in light that's always new.
